01 / Where wildlife needs care

Many animals.
Different needs.

In 2024, the public and government agencies handed over 16,034 animals to PERHILITAN. Most were reptiles. But the mix varied between reporting states.

Both maps show Peninsular Malaysia. Sabah and Sarawak are outside this PERHILITAN table’s coverage. The combined Federal Territories total (1,038) is included in the overall count but cannot be assigned to individual territory polygons; those areas are grey.

Source: PERHILITAN Annual Report 2024, Table 34, p121. Boundaries: geoBoundaries / OpenStreetMap contributors. Hover over a state or circle for details.

03 / Back to the forest

A release has a destination.
Its outcome takes longer to know.

NWRC’s 2024 report lists 32 released animals across 16 species and six named destinations. These records document release, without establishing what happened to every animal afterwards.

Beyond the rescue centre / Tun Mustapha Park, Sabah

Many hands, a shared restoration effort.

WWF-Malaysia reports that a 2024 coral-planting event led by Sabah Parks involved 500 volunteers, with 12,000 coral fragments planted on 1,000 reef stars. WWF supported training, equipment and community mobilisation.

One collaborative event—not annual national totals or proof of long-term coral survival.

Source: WWF-Malaysia Annual Review 2025, p21.

Care is more than a rescue count.

It is a sequence of decisions,
places and people.

These reports make parts of that work visible. They also remind us that an intake, a release and a volunteer placement each tell a different part of the story.

How to interpret these figures
  • Handovers are reported administrative records, not estimates of wild populations or state-level conservation performance.
  • NWRC intake and release figures do not follow the same intake cohort. Dividing releases by intake would not give a valid rehabilitation success rate.
  • BSBCC care milestones describe selected individuals, not every bear’s experience.
  • Placement days measure calendar participation, including possible rest or travel days. They cannot establish outcomes caused by volunteers.
  • The full extraction notes and source locators are in the data provenance record.
